01 • CLUB HISTORY

A century of football history.

Real Madrid has developed from a Madrid football club into one of the most recognised names in world football.

From its foundation in 1902 to its European triumphs and generations of legendary players, the club has built a history that continues to inspire millions of supporters.

1902 Founded
1956 First European Cup
15 European Cups
1956

The beginning of a European legacy

Real Madrid won the first European Cup in 1956. The club then continued its European success by winning the competition five times in a row from 1956 to 1960.

02 • CLUB HISTORY

Moments of Glory

Important moments that helped shape the history and identity of Real Madrid.

1902

The Foundation

Real Madrid was founded in 1902 and gradually became one of the most famous football clubs in the world.

1956

First European Cup

Real Madrid won the first European Cup in 1956, beginning a remarkable European football legacy.

1956–60

Five in a Row

Real Madrid won the first five European Cups consecutively between 1956 and 1960.

1960

European Dominance

Real Madrid defeated Eintracht Frankfurt 7–3 in the 1960 European Cup final.

2000

Club of the Century

FIFA named Real Madrid the Best Club of the 20th Century in 2000.

2014

La Décima

In 2014, Real Madrid won its tenth European Cup, widely known as La Décima.
